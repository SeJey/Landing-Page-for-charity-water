The Crisis

While We Scroll,
They Walk Miles.

In the time it takes to finish a lecture, a young girl in Ethiopia walks 6 miles to collect water that will likely make her sick. That's not a distant problem — it's a solvable one.

Waterborne diseases claim thousands of lives every week. Lives that could be saved for the same cost as a monthly coffee habit. charity: water has cracked the code — and you can be part of it.

🦠

Dirty Water Kills

More children die from waterborne diseases than AIDS, malaria, and measles combined. Clean water is the most basic form of healthcare.

📚

Girls Miss School

When a nearby well is built, girls' school enrollment jumps up to 15%. Water access equals education access equals breaking generational poverty.

⏰

200M Hours Stolen Daily

Every day, 200 million hours are spent collecting water — mostly by women and girls. That's time stolen from school, work, and dreams.
